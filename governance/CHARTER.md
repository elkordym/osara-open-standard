# Open Standard Charter

## Mission

Develop and maintain OSARA and companion governance texts as open standards that protect human rights, sovereignty, safety, interoperability, and portability in AI agent ecosystems.

## Scope

This project stewards:

- OSARA technical architecture and compliance requirements
- AI Bill of Rights policy and rights framework
- Cross-reference mappings between rights and technical controls
- Public consultation and change management process

## Governance Model

- **Maintainers Council**: approves releases, resolves deadlocks, and enforces process.
- **Editors**: maintain text quality, consistency, and versioned publication.
- **Contributors**: propose issues, pull requests, and public comments.

## Decision Process

- Minor editorial changes: editor approval
- Normative requirement changes: maintainers council approval
- Breaking changes to rights or compliance model: public comment period required

## Transparency Rules

- All proposals must be tracked in issues or pull requests.
- All accepted normative changes must include rationale.
- Rejected major proposals must include a short written reason.

## Versioning Policy

- Drafts: `vX.Y-draft.N`
- Stable releases: `vX.Y`
- Patch clarifications (non-normative): `vX.Y.Z`
