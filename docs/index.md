This site publishes working drafts and governance material for the **OSARA Standards Family**, produced by [Open Source United](https://opensource.unicc.org/) — a Community of Practice of the United Nations.

## The Standards Family

| Document | Role | Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OSARA Overview & Concepts](specs/osara-overview/current.md) | Informative | Executives, policymakers, journalists, civil society — start here |
| [OSARA Specification](specs/osara/current.md) | **Normative** | Implementers, integrators |
| [AI Bill of Rights](specs/ai-bill-of-rights/current.md) | Policy | Policymakers, regulators, civil society |
| [OSARA Threat Model](specs/osara-threat-model/current.md) | Informative | Auditors, security engineers |
| [OSARA Privacy Considerations](specs/osara-privacy/current.md) | Informative | DPOs, regulators, auditors |

## What You Can Do Here

- Read current draft texts of the OSARA Specification and the AI Bill of Rights.
- Read the informative companions: Overview, Threat Model, Privacy Considerations.
- Track the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md), [Release Process](RELEASE_PROCESS.md), and [Versioning](VERSIONING.md).
- Participate through public comments and RFCs — see the [Community Communication guide](COMMUNITY_COMMUNICATION.md).
- File [errata](errata.md) and review the [Public Comments Register](comments-register.md).
- Follow transparent governance decisions in [governance/](governance/CHARTER.md).

## Start Here

- Read [`getting-started.md`](getting-started.md).
- Review [`community/CONTRIBUTING.md`](community/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [IPR Policy](governance/IPR_POLICY.md).
- Open a proposal or comment in the repository issue tracker using the **Public Comment** or **Spec Change** templates.
