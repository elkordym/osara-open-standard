# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to an open, respectful, and safe collaboration environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, geography, disability, or technical level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification
- Critique ideas, not people
- Keep discussions evidence-based and on-topic

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or bad-faith derailment
- Publishing private information without consent
- Repeated spam or disruption

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, lock threads, or suspend participation when behavior violates this policy.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in `governance/MAINTAINERS.md`.
